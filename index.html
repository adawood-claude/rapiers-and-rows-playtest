What to do

  1. Pick a Story difficulty on the menu (Cadet is the standard game), then play 1.1 The Courtyard Ambush three times and Endless twice. If you have time, try 1.2 Overturned Tables too.
  2. When a game ends, take a screenshot of the results screen.
  3. Send the screenshots to Ali with short answers to the questions below.

Questions

  • Were the controls clear? Did pieces ever move, turn or drop when you didn't mean them to?
  • Could you tell blades, firearms and hats apart at a glance? Which art style did you like best?
  • Did you want to play another round? What made you stop?
  • Which difficulty did you play? Did it feel too easy, right, or too hard?
  • If you played in Spanish or Thai, did anything read oddly or feel wrongly translated?

Controls

  • Drag sideways on the board, or the strip below it, to move.
  • Tap the right half to rotate clockwise, the left half to turn back.
  • Drag down slowly to ease a piece down. Flick down to drop it.
  • Tap an Armory slot to choose your next piece. Tap the Cannon button when it glows to fire a cannonball. After 3 Cannon shots, tap Broadside, then FIRE! as the side cannon passes the rows you want gone.

Sound starts after your first tap. Best on a phone held upright.
